# Changelog

## 0.2.0

- **Answer in the sidebar:** the Live card shows what the agent is doing and the current question
  while hyperfocus runs in this folder. Answer by clicking or with `1`–`9`; ask a follow-up, save to
  your notebook, rate a bad question, keep going or go back to the agent. Needs hyperfocus 0.7.0.
- **Jump to the code** a question is about, highlighted.
- **Gutter marks** on lines you were quizzed on, with the question on hover.
- **Review staged changes** from the Source Control view, no agent needed.
- **Notebook** tree view of your saved questions.
- **Status bar item** and a notification when the agent finishes.
- **Start hyperfocus** finds your installed agents, asks once and remembers; a walkthrough gets you
  to your first question.

## 0.1.0

- Stats, weak spots, saved and recently missed questions in a sidebar; start hyperfocus in a terminal.
