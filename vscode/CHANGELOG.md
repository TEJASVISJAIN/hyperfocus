# Changelog

## 0.2.0

- **Questions in the sidebar:** while your agent works, the question about its change appears in the
  panel. Click an answer or press `1`–`9`, then Next, Save or ask a follow-up. While the panel is open
  the terminal stays on your agent. Needs hyperfocus 0.7.0.
- **A simpler panel:** what hyperfocus is and one Start button when nothing runs; just the question
  while your agent works; your progress and saved questions underneath once there are some.
- **Start** runs the agent you have installed, with no questions asked.
- **Jump to the code** a question is about, highlighted.
- **Gutter marks** on lines you were quizzed on, with the question on hover.
- **Review staged changes** from the Source Control view, no agent needed.
- **Status bar item** and a notification when the agent finishes.

## 0.1.0

- Stats, weak spots, saved and recently missed questions in a sidebar; start hyperfocus in a terminal.
